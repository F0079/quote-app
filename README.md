# Quoted

**Quoted** turns a quote into a beautiful image you can save and share — right on your
Android phone. Pick a photo or a colour, add a quote and your own styling, and export a
ready-to-post picture. Everything happens on your device: no account, no cloud, no network.

> Free, open source, and built with the help of AI.

## What it is

Quoted is a small creative app for making quote cards. You get a single canvas where you
combine a photo or a colour, a quote, and an optional frame. Nothing is forced: add the
pieces in any order and save or export whenever you like.

## Features

Everything below is grouped by feature topic.

### Workspace & Canvas
- One canvas is the single working area
- Start empty and add photo, quote, and frame in any order
- Background is either a photo or a solid colour
- Choose the aspect ratio at any time — original, square, portrait, landscape — with or
  without a photo
- Save or export at any moment, even an empty canvas

### Photo
- Take a photo with the camera (full resolution)
- Pick an image from your gallery
- Crop the photo
- Supports JPEG and PNG input (up to 5 MB)

### Quote & Author
- Separate fields for the quote (up to 512 characters) and the author
- 8+ built-in fonts plus the system font
- Free colour palette
- Adjustable font size
- Bold and italic, set separately for quote and author
- Horizontal alignment (left, centre, right) and vertical placement (top, middle, bottom)
- Line spacing
- Text width as a percentage of the canvas, controlling the line break
- Quotation marks as normal punctuation, large decorative marks, or hidden

### Frames
- "No frame" (default) plus 6+ styles: Vintage, Minimalist, Gold, Silver, Nature, Fancy
- Adjustable frame colour
- Adjustable frame width (about 5%–30%)

### Image Effects & Readability
- Lighten/darken, blur, black & white, warm/cool colour, and vignette — each with adjustable
  strength
- Text shadow, text outline, and a translucent backdrop behind the text
- Background gradient, working with or without a photo
- Effects and readability aids coexist with frames: frames are kept, not replaced

### Text Styles & Templates
- Save reusable text styles (font, size, colour, weight, spacing, and readability aids) and
  apply them across projects
- Built-in recommended templates set the aspect ratio, element positions, and text styles
- Content stays editable after applying a template; templates don't change background or frame

### Quote Collection
- Built-in quote collection that works fully offline
- Browse it in an editor sub-view and tap a quote to place it on the canvas
- Search by quote text or author
- Filter by topic (for example calmness, courage, transience, self-determination)
- Favourites and recently used
- Random quote ("discover")
- Report mistakes or suggest new quotes via an external link

### Editor Comfort
- Drag the quote and author to position them
- Alignment guides while dragging
- Undo and redo
- Automatic saving while you edit
- Overflow warning when text exceeds the set width — no silent truncation

### Library
- All saved cards in one grid
- Reopen a card and keep editing
- Rename, duplicate, and delete
- Creation and last-modified dates
- Each project keeps its own copy of the photo, independent of the original gallery image

### Export & Share
- JPG and PNG with an adjustable quality setting (60–100%)
- Choose an export size (long edge 1080 / 1440 / 2048 px); the app shows the actual
  resolution you'll get
- No watermark
- Share directly to WhatsApp, Instagram, and other apps

### Setup, Theme & Privacy
- First-run setup to choose your export folder and theme
- Light and dark theme, following your system setting automatically, with a manual switch
- 100% local and offline: no cloud sync and no account
- Runs on Android 7.0 and newer

### Availability
- Distribution planned for Google Play, F-Droid, Obtainium (via GitHub releases), and a
  direct APK download from the website
- No Google Play Services or other proprietary dependencies, so it stays available on
  alternative stores

## Open source, built with AI

Quoted is free and open source — there are no in-app purchases. The project is being
implemented with the help of AI: development uses **OpenCode**, an open-source AI coding agent.
